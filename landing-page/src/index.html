A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity starts
with questions.

Get to know Searix before your first trade.

What is Searix Trade?

Searix is a mobile trading companion for supported Solana markets. It brings market discovery, execution-quality context, pre-trade previews and saved receipts into one workflow.

Does Searix hold my funds or private keys?

The app uses your connected wallet to sign transactions. Searix does not ask for or store your private key. Always review the transaction presented by your wallet.

Can I trade on this website?

No. This is a product landing page with an illustrative fee preview. Trading and wallet signing belong to the mobile app; the current signing integration is Android-focused.

Is the app publicly available?

Searix is a hackathon-stage project. A public download link has not been announced on this page. Explore the product flow here while the pilot is being prepared.

Do quality signals guarantee a good trade?

No. They provide market context, not financial advice or a guarantee of execution quality, asset safety or returns. Live conditions can change between a preview and execution.
